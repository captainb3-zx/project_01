About this project

Chronic diseases develop slowly, often silently, and are largely preventable. Through surveys, screening awareness and data analysis, we aim to:

Study

Collect and analyse data on the incidence of diabetes, hypertension and related conditions in the community we surveyed.

Educate

Spread awareness about risk factors, early symptoms and the importance of regular check-ups.

Support

Encourage healthy habits and guide people to nearby health facilities for screening.

Apply engineering

Use data handling, visualisation and simple tools to turn survey numbers into insight.

Chronic diseases we looked at

Many of these share the same causes: unhealthy diet, inactivity, tobacco, alcohol and stress.

Diabetes

Blood sugar stays too high. Signs: frequent urination, thirst, fatigue, slow-healing wounds, blurred vision. Can damage eyes, kidneys, nerves and heart.

Hypertension

Persistently high blood pressure (140/90 mmHg or more). Often no symptoms, hence the “silent killer”. Raises risk of stroke and heart attack.

Heart disease

Includes coronary artery disease. Linked to high cholesterol, smoking, diabetes and obesity.

Obesity

Excess body fat, especially around the waist, is a major driver of diabetes and heart disease.

Chronic respiratory disease

COPD and asthma, driven by smoking, pollution and occupational dust.

Chronic kidney disease

Often a complication of long-standing diabetes and high blood pressure.

Prevention: small habits, big impact

Eat smart

  • More vegetables, pulses, whole grains
  • Cut sugary drinks and sweets
  • Limit fried and packaged food
  • Reduce salt

Move daily

  • At least 150 minutes of brisk activity a week
  • Take stairs, walk after meals
  • Break long sitting with short walks

Avoid harm

  • No tobacco
  • Limit alcohol
  • Sleep 7 to 8 hours
  • Manage stress

Get screened

  • Check blood sugar and BP yearly after 30
  • Earlier if overweight or family history
  • Follow doctor’s advice if diagnosed
